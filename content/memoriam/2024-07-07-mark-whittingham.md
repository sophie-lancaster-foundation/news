Mark Whittingham, ”Brother Whitters” to many, passed away unexpectedly on the 7 July.

Mark was a creative person who worked in the field of graphic design throughout his life. He had a love for design, books and music, especially punk music. The gigs he went to are countless, and he was a well-known face at the Rebellion Punk Festival every year. He also loved rambling around charity shops in Birmingham and beyond, always looking for a bargain. As a result Xmas presents were always a lottery but never dull.

Mark will be remembered as a warm and loving man with a heart of gold, a generous person who gave of himself and always took the time to brighten the day of everyone around him. His brilliant way with words, along with a great sense of humour and witty remarks, gave us many good laughs.

Through The Sophie Lancaster Foundation we hope to give back a little of all Mark gave to us. It was a charity he related to and he wore his Sophie wristband with pride. Hopefully he’s looking down on us, well chuffed, with that infectious smile of his.

To anyone reading this, struggling and not sharing, you need to share…there’s more help out there through friends, family and charities…you’re never alone.

Mark, you were more respected and more loved than you probably ever realised, and you are missed beyond words. Thank you for everything.

There’s always the sun ☀️
