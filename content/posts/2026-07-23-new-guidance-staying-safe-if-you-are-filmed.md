We are increasingly seeing videos shared online that appear to target people simply because they are perceived as different or vulnerable. Some content creators deliberately film members of the public to mock, harass or humiliate them for online views and engagement. In some cases, the videos may also reveal personal information or encourage others to target the individual.

This guidance explains practical steps you can take to protect yourself, safeguard your personal information and know what to do if you believe you have been deliberately targeted.
